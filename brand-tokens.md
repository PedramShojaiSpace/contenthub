## Typography

### Headlines
- **Font**: Raisonne Pro Regular (geometric sans-serif)
- **Fallback**: 'Sofia Pro', 'Futura', sans-serif
- **Web substitute**: 'DM Sans' or 'Nunito' (closest free Google Font)
- **Case**: Sentence case (NOT all caps, NOT title case for display)
- **Size**: Large — maximize visual impact
- **Hyphenation**: Never

### Body Copy
- **Font**: Sofia Pro
- **Fallback**: 'Arial', sans-serif
- **Web substitute**: 'Sofia Sans' (Google Fonts) or 'Inter'
- **Case**: Sentence case (upper and lowercase)
- **Weight**: Regular / Bold / Italic / Bold Italic

## Social Post Design System

### Message Posts (Quote/Insight cards)
- **Background**: Solid brand color (Fire #ed5939, Wood #3d7e51, Water #5870aa, Earth #f6a032)
- **Text**: White, centered, sentence case
- **Font**: Raisonne Pro / Sofia Pro
- **Graphic**: Abstract "Life Garden" toolkit element (faint, same hue, lighter/darker tone)
- **Logo**: Urban Monk icon (circle with infinity/wave) bottom-right corner, white
- **Layout**: Text centered vertically and horizontally

### Brand Campaign Posts
- **Layout**: Split — 1/2 image, 1/2 message (alternating vertical/horizontal)
- **Background**: Metal (#f7f4ef) cream on the text half
- **Text**: Yin (#161513) dark, sentence case
- **Graphics**: Life Garden toolkit elements scattered decoratively
- **Logo**: Urban Monk icon bottom-right, brand color

## Carousel / Slide Design

### Key Principles
- Solid brand color backgrounds (rotate through Fire, Wood, Water, Earth)
- White text on colored backgrounds
- Dark text (#161513) on cream/white backgrounds
- Urban Monk logo mark in bottom-right corner
- Abstract graphic elements (dots, lines, circles) as decorative watermarks
- Clean, minimal layout — large headline, short body text
- No gradients — flat solid colors only

### Slide Types
1. **Cover**: Large headline centered, brand color bg, white text, logo mark
2. **Content**: Headline top, bullet points or short body, decorative element, logo
3. **CTA**: Cream bg (#f7f4ef), dark text, Fire-colored accent, URL/call to action

## Logo Mark
- Circle containing: sun/dot (top), mountain waves (middle), infinity symbol (bottom)
- Used as watermark/stamp in bottom-right of all social posts
- Colors: Yin (dark bg), Fire (light bg or brand color bg), White (dark bg)
- Minimum size: 30% of shortest side

## Graphic Elements ("Life Garden Toolkit")
- Color Dots: Earth-01/02, Fire-01/02, Wood-01/02, Water-01/02
- Paint elements: Seeds (dot clusters), Paths (brushstrokes), Mountains (zigzag), Crops (oval rings)
- Sketch elements: Moon (circle), Forward (arrows), Journey (wave), Plant (leaf), Levels (lines), Path, Process (spirals)
- Usage: Decorative watermarks, light/dark tone of bg color

## Design Principles
- Flat, organic, hand-crafted aesthetic
- Inspired by Taoist five elements: Wood, Fire, Water, Earth, Metal + Yin/Yang
- Warm cream (#f7f4ef) as primary background
- Bold solid color blocks for emphasis
- Generous white space
- Never use gradients
- Never use drop shadows on type

The joke in conventional medicine is that there is an "ologist" for everything.

You go to the rheumatologist for your joints, the neurologist for your nerves, the cardiologist for your heart, and the psychologist for your mood.

Your internist is basically a quarterback that isn't allowed to do much and just has to send you to the specialists downstream, where you have the distinct pleasure of sitting in yet another waiting room, waiting for yet another person to spend maybe 7 minutes with you.

I can't tell you how many thousands of patients have come to me complaining about how frustrating this is.

It's dehumanizing and an incredibly disrespectful way to have someone spend their time.

It would all be worth it if you felt great, but there's the rub.

Most people are getting sicker and sicker and being put on more and more drugs despite all their efforts to go visit these “ologists.” Many of my patients have complained that they see the specialists more often than they see their loved ones.

If you can relate, you're not alone.

Here's an important conversation I have had recently with a number of our patients about this very subject.

There is a way out of this mess, but it's going to involve you.

It's time for you to take a more active role in your healing, and on the other side of this comes wisdom, knowledge, and, yes, better results.

Upstream: The Complete Microbiome Solution is a deeper, six-week educational experience for people ready to move from a broad understanding of health into a more structured exploration of the oral–gut connection and the foundations of microbiome resilience.

Led by Dr. Pedram Shojai with educational contributions from Dr. Jeff Bland, Upstream gives you an organized framework for understanding the inputs that influence how you feel—and for choosing more intentional next steps.

Inside Upstream

  • A six-week guided course designed to be completed at your own pace.
  • The 6R framework for thinking through foundational patterns that affect the microbiome.
  • In-depth educational lessons that build on the themes introduced in Interconnected.
  • Expert perspective from Dr. Pedram Shojai and Dr. Jeff Bland.

Interconnected is complete on its own. Upstream is the optional next layer for anyone who wants a deeper curriculum and a more organized learning path.

Educational content only. Upstream is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.

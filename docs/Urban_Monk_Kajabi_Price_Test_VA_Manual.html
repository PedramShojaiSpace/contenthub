STOP BEFORE YOU START
This manual authorizes only the creation and configuration of two draft Offers. It does not authorize publishing, sharing checkout links, activating a traffic split, exposing a test price, sending any email or SMS, changing Meta, changing a landing page, or taking a test payment.

If any screen appears different from this guide, stop and ask the owner for direction.

1. Purpose and non-negotiable boundaries

The Academy is preparing a controlled entry-price experiment for the Interconnected offer. This manual shows how to create two draft Offers at $49 and $99 while preserving the existing $67 control. The only intended difference across the three arms is the entry price. Do not make a customer-facing copy, product-access, checkout, upsell, email, automation, or channel change while completing this work.

You MAY doYou MUST NOT do
• Create a new Kajabi Offer in Draft status.
• Attach the same Interconnected product as the $67 control.
• Set a one-time USD price of $49 or $99.
• Mirror the $67 control’s purchase flow only where the screens clearly confirm the same setting.
• Capture Offer IDs, URLs, and screenshots for owner review.		• Publish either new Offer or change the live $67 control.
• Click Activate New Checkouts, Enable, Publish, Go Live, or Share.
• Create a coupon or a multi-price choice on one Offer.
• Activate a split, route traffic, change a landing page, or edit UTM/tracking/pixel settings.
• Edit Klaviyo, email, SMS, Meta campaigns, audiences, ads, or budgets.
• Place a test purchase without separate explicit owner approval.
Hard stop rule
If Kajabi asks you to activate a checkout template, publish an Offer, make a payment, send a message, enroll a contact, or turn on a traffic experience, do not proceed. Take a screenshot and send it to the owner.

5. Detailed procedure — create the $49 draft Offer

  1. Log in to Kajabi and confirm the selected site is The Urban Monk Academy.
  2. Go to Sales → Pricing. Do not use the global Activate New Checkouts button.
  3. Find the existing published control, Interconnected $67 Bundle OTO. Open it only to read the Details, Pricing, and Purchase flow settings. Do not press Save.
  4. Return to Sales → Pricing and choose New Offer.
  5. For the customer-facing Offer title, enter: Interconnected: The Complete Healing Protocol. Do not include “test,” “$49,” or “P1” in the customer-facing title.
  6. Select Interconnected Series Self Guided as the only product. Confirm the product count is one before continuing.
  7. Set the internal title to: Interconnected $49 Bundle OTO — Price Test P1.
  8. Set payment type to One-time Payment, currency to USD, and price to 49.00. Do not add another pricing option, coupon, subscription, payment plan, or pay-what-you-want setting.
  9. Compare remaining pricing and checkout options to the $67 control. Match the control exactly. If you cannot verify a setting, leave the new Offer in Draft and flag it for the owner instead of guessing.
  10. Keep Offer Status set to Draft. Save the draft only. Do not publish, activate a checkout template, copy/share a customer link, or route traffic.

7. Create the $99 draft Offer

Repeat the same workflow for the $99 draft. The customer-facing title, product access, checkout configuration, post-purchase page, email preference, upsell, and automation must match the $67 control. The only intended differences are the entry price and internal title.

  1. Return to Sales → Pricing and select New Offer.
  2. Use customer-facing title: Interconnected: The Complete Healing Protocol.
  3. Attach only Interconnected Series Self Guided.
  4. Set internal title: Interconnected $99 Bundle OTO — Price Test P2.
  5. Set One-time Payment, USD, and price 99.00.
  6. Mirror the $67 control Purchase flow using the same verification table in Section 6.
  7. Keep this Offer in Draft. This is preparation only; the $99 test does not run until Phase 1 completes and the owner separately approves Phase 2.

10. What happens next — not the VA’s action

After the owner reviews the completed handoff record, a separate approval is required before any test becomes customer-facing. The owner must approve the exact $49 Offer ID and checkout URL, the intended $199 upsell treatment, the 50/50 $49-versus-$67 Phase 1 allocation, the test start/end window, the tracking/attribution setup, and the person responsible for activation.

The $99 Offer remains draft-only until the owner reviews the $49-versus-$67 result and separately approves a second phase. The VA must not activate a split, publish an Offer, turn on a new checkout, share a customer checkout link, or tell the ad buyer to change spend.

11. Questions to ask the owner instead of making an assumption

  • I cannot find the exact Interconnected Series Self Guided product.
  • Kajabi shows more than one $199 upsell or a different upsell name/price.
  • A setting on the $67 control is unclear or unavailable on the new draft Offer.
  • The page asks me to activate a checkout template or publish the Offer.
  • The new Offer appears to have different tax, payment, quantity, or custom-price-text settings.
  • I cannot prove the post-purchase page, email preference, upsell, or automation matches the control.
  • Anyone asks me to send a link, share a price, add people to a list, change Klaviyo/SMS, or adjust Meta traffic.

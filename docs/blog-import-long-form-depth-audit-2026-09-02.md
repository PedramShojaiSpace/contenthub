The article is therefore a useful example of why imported vlogs and short source drafts must be treated as an initial editorial brief rather than a near-final post. The public page also presents a related-post preview below the article; that preview is theme content, not part of post `11464`’s article body.

## New system standard

Blog Import Studio now requires at least **1,200 words before the CTA**, with a target band of **1,400–1,900 words**. It requires a substantive opening, at least five distinct H2 sections, contextual explanation, modern-life application, carefully bounded practical considerations, a limits/uncertainty section, and a 3–5 question FAQ. The workflow specifically requires spiritual, traditional, esoteric, and metaphorical claims to be framed as perspectives rather than settled biological science, and it prohibits invented research, citations, quotations, testimonials, patient stories, or health guarantees.

The Studio exposes a live word-count gate at review time. A draft cannot be saved or handed to WordPress/Substack below the minimum. This is a workflow improvement for future imports; upgrading post `11464` itself remains an exact-scope, owner-approved WordPress action.

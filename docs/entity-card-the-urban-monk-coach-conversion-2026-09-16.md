## Analytical boundaries

The analysis attributes paid Shopify orders to a coach only where the order customer can be matched to a Calendly invitee and the purchase occurs within the documented post-appointment attribution window. Calendly active events represent scheduled/held-event proxies; cancellation, no-show, reschedule, and attendance status are not reliably available in the supplied endpoint and will be reported as a limitation rather than inferred.

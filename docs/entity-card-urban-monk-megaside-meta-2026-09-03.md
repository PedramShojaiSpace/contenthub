# Entity Card — Urban Monk and MEGA/MegaSide Meta Advertising Inventory

**Reference date:** September 3, 2026, America/Chicago.  
**Purpose:** Establish the defined entity and account-coverage boundary for a read-only inventory of ads with current-day spend.

| Field | Urban Monk | MEGA/MegaSide |
|---|---|---|
| Common name | The Urban Monk / Urban Monk | MEGA / MegaSide (owner-provided operating label) |
| Listing status | Private operating brand; no public ticker requested | Private operating label; no public ticker requested |
| Reporting currency | USD where returned by Meta | USD where returned by Meta |
| Data universe | Every Meta ad account accessible through the current authorized business/account reporting paths and visibly associated with Urban Monk | Every Meta ad account accessible through the current authorized business/account reporting paths and visibly associated with MEGA/MegaSide |
| Time boundary | Today only, based on `America/Chicago` calendar dates | Today only, based on `America/Chicago` calendar dates |
| Inclusion rule | An ad with current-day `spend > 0`; active delivery status reported alongside spend where available | An ad with current-day `spend > 0`; active delivery status reported alongside spend where available |
| Exclusion rule | Paused, archived, draft, or zero-spend ads are excluded from the primary spending list but may be used only to explain coverage | Paused, archived, draft, or zero-spend ads are excluded from the primary spending list but may be used only to explain coverage |

> This is an operational advertising-spend inventory, not investment analysis. It will not change any Meta object. “All accounts” means all accounts discoverable and readable through the currently authorized Meta access paths; any inaccessible business portfolio or account will be reported as a coverage limitation rather than assumed empty.

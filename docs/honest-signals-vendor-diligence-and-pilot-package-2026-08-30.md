# Honest Signals Vendor-Diligence and Pilot Package

**Prepared for:** The Urban Monk  
**Purpose:** Evaluate Honest Signals as a possible managed messaging/creative-test partner without granting account access, transferring customer data, changing advertising, or committing to a pilot.  
**Current vendor status:** No vendor account connection, software integration, data exchange, campaign change, audience action, or commercial commitment has been made.

## Cover email / message draft

> **Subject:** Urban Monk — messaging-test methodology and controlled pilot diligence
>
> Hello Honest Signals team,
>
> We are evaluating a tightly controlled paid-social messaging/creative test for The Urban Monk. We are interested in your advertised message-cluster and scientific-elimination approach. Before considering a pilot, we need to understand the testing design, platform access, measurement framework, data handling, and commercial scope.
>
> We will not provide raw customer lists, email addresses, health information, medical/test records, patient information, CRM exports, or credentials during initial diligence. We are open to sharing a sanitized brand/funnel brief and aggregate historical performance only. If a pilot proceeds, the initial test would be one offer, one working destination, one fixed audience strategy, and multiple policy-reviewed creative/message variants. Campaign publication and any account change would remain under our explicit approval.
>
> Please respond to the questions below and provide the supporting documents requested. We are looking for a practical, evidence-led answer rather than generic claims.
>
> Thank you,
>
> The Urban Monk Team

## Data, privacy, and legal boundary

The initial diligence and pilot-design stage must remain **aggregate-only**. The vendor may receive a sanitized creative/funnel brief, approved destination URL, policy requirements, and aggregate campaign/funnel results. The vendor must not receive raw customer, lead, employee, audience, or prospect data.

| Data / access category | Initial diligence | Optional later pilot only after written approval | Not permitted in this evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer and public landing-page details | Allowed. | Allowed. | N/A |
| Aggregate performance totals | Allowed, with source/coverage noted. | Allowed. | N/A |
| Ad-account access | Not granted. | Prefer limited, revocable read-only access; manual aggregate exports remain the default. | Admin access without an approved access register and written need. |
| Meta Pixel / Conversions API access | Not granted. | Only after a written asset/permission map and separate approval. | Sharing server tokens, unrestricted asset control, or undocumented subprocessor access. |
| Apollo business contacts / Custom Audience files | Not shared. | Not shared for this pilot. | Any raw or hashed customer-list transfer to Honest Signals. |
| Kajabi, Klaviyo, Sendy, Shopify, or WordPress contact records | Not shared. | Not shared unless a separate data-protection review and explicit approval occurs. | Any uncontrolled CRM/email-system connection. |
| Health, medical, test, symptom, supplement, or patient data | Never shared. | Never shared. | Any direct/indirect upload, enrichment, or modeling use. |

If Honest Signals will process any Urban Monk non-public data later, require a mutually acceptable agreement and data-processing addendum, including a field-level data map, permitted-purpose statement, processing location, security controls, subprocessors, retention limit, data-return/deletion process, breach-notice terms, and audit/right-to-verify language. This document is operational diligence, not legal advice.

## Access and asset-control checklist

The Urban Monk team keeps ownership and control of all advertising assets. No vendor access should be granted during diligence. If a later pilot genuinely requires access, grant the least privilege for the shortest documented period and retain a revocation record.

| Control | Required position |
|---|---|
| Business ownership | Urban Monk Productions, Inc. retains ownership and administrator control of every Meta business asset. |
| Role scope | Use the minimum role needed; begin with manual reports or read-only access if the vendor can operate that way. |
| Credentials | Never give password, recovery code, system-user token, API key, tracking token, or customer-list file to the vendor. |
| Assets | Grant no access to customer lists, Custom Audiences, payment methods, Business Settings, Pixel/CAPI configuration, or automation controls during the evaluation. |
| Review | Record who approved access, what was granted, why, when it expires, and how it will be revoked. |
| Creative and data ownership | Urban Monk retains unrestricted ownership/use of its creative, briefs, test results, message library, and first-party performance data. |
| Exit | Require written confirmation of access removal and data deletion at pilot end. |

## Controlled pilot scorecard

The pilot is **not yet approved**. The scorecard below supplies the acceptance criteria for a potential manual test. It uses the Signal Lab and existing Content Hub attribution reporting, not an automatic campaign-control system.

| Dimension | Baseline to capture before any test | Test comparison | Decision use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Experiment definition | Offer, destination route, audience strategy, optimization event, attribution view, placements, budget/exposure cap, and date window. | Only message/creative varies. | Protects causal interpretation. |
| Leading delivery | Spend, impressions, frequency, CPM, outbound CTR, landing-page views. | Variant vs. control. | Directional only; never a stand-alone winner. |
| First-party intent | Landing-page-view rate, lead-start rate, lead completion rate, cost per lead. | Variant vs. control. | Detects traffic/offer mismatch and form friction. |
| Quality | Predefined qualified-lead rate, checkout arrival rate, checkout completion rate. | Variant vs. control. | Confirms whether attention carries below the form. |
| Economics | Confirmed purchase count, revenue, cost per purchase, revenue per lead, ROAS/contribution where coverage is complete. | Variant vs. control. | Primary decision layer. |
| Measurement integrity | Meta-reported events, first-party observed events, matching/coverage note, and reconciliation exceptions. | Complete / partial / not connected. | Prevents false certainty in attribution. |
| Policy and customer experience | Review status, rejection/appeal record, landing-page consistency, customer feedback where available. | Must clear for every variant. | A weak policy/customer experience disqualifies a “winner.” |

### Pilot acceptance criteria

The vendor should agree in writing that no result will be represented as a scalable success merely because CTR or CPL improves. A test is eligible for a next-step decision only after the agreed measurement window closes, the exact test variables are documented, data coverage is visible, and the team can compare confirmed downstream outcomes. Meta itself recommends using formal A/B testing rather than informal on/off changes when comparing strategies, and its current creative-test product supports 2–7 creative variants in an existing campaign under specified conditions.[1] [2]

The initial test must use ordinary benefit-led messaging and avoid asserting or implying a viewer’s health, mental-health, diagnosis, treatment, or other personal attribute. Meta describes these as prohibited personal-attribute approaches and tells advertisers to focus on product/service benefits.[3] The ad must accurately reflect the landing-page offer and set realistic expectations; Meta’s unacceptable-business-practices guidance specifically calls for a cohesive ad-to-landing experience.[4]

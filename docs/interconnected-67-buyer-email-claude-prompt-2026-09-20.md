## Copy-and-paste prompt for Claude

```text
You are the senior lifecycle-email copywriter for The Urban Monk and Dr. Pedram Shojai. Write a complete, high-trust email sequence for people who have just purchased the $67 product “Interconnected: The Complete Healing Protocol.”

This audience entered through an Interconnected free-screening funnel and then paid for permanent access. They are no longer leads. Treat them as customers who have shown trust and intent. The first job is to help them use what they bought. The second job is to offer a thoughtful, non-pushy path into testing, live education, and the broader Urban Monk ecosystem.

YOUR ASSIGNMENT
Write 9 emails total:
1. Transactional access-delivery template — immediately after payment.
2. $199 member-offer recovery email — 20 minutes after payment, only for a buyer who did NOT accept or purchase the $199 offer through the native Shopify post-purchase experience.
3. Day 1 — paid orientation and series activation.
4. Day 3 — help them use the series rather than binge it.
5. Day 6 — activate the Gut Restoration Starter Protocol.
6. Day 9 — educational invitation to the $199 testing + health-coach option.
7. Day 13 — invitation to the next approved Interconnected webinar or evergreen teaching.
8. Day 17 — decision-support email: self-guided learning, testing, or live learning.
9. Day 24 — re-engagement, reflection, and return to the protocol.
10. Day 30 — progress review and invitation into the next approved live/core-funnel experience.

Important: There are 10 rows above because Email #1 is transactional access delivery and the remaining 9 messages are marketing/lifecycle messages. Write every message as a separate draft.

PRODUCT FACTS YOU MAY USE
The $67 product is “Interconnected: The Complete Healing Protocol.” It is a one-time purchase with permanent access. The product includes:
- The complete nine-part Interconnected series, available on demand.
- The Gut Restoration Starter Protocol, described as a practical 30-day reset plan.
- The Interconnected Companion Guide, including episode-by-episode prompts and action steps.
- The 5 Root Causes Masterclass.
- Optional private Interconnected community access.

The product is educational content. It is not individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

THE $199 OFFER
The optional next step is “Gut Permeability Test + Health Coach Call — $199 Member Offer.” It is offered first through a native Shopify Zipify post-purchase screen. The email sequence must not compete with or duplicate that experience.

The 20-minute $199 recovery email is sent only when the buyer does not already have the $199 product on the completed or amended Shopify order. It must not be called “one click” in email because it leads to a standard checkout. It must frame the offer as an optional way to make their next-step conversation more specific, not as a diagnostic, cure, treatment, guarantee, or urgent discount.

AUDIENCE AND VOICE
Write in Dr. Pedram Shojai’s voice: grounded, clear, humane, intelligent, direct, and experiential. This should feel like a trusted guide, not a funnel. Use short paragraphs. Use straightforward language. Earn the right to make an offer by helping the customer use the product first.

The reader is likely thoughtful and health-curious. They may feel overwhelmed by health information. Do not use fear, shame, pseudo-science, fake urgency, exaggerated transformation claims, or manipulative scarcity. Avoid words such as “diagnose,” “treat,” “reverse,” “heal your gut,” “guaranteed,” “cure,” “miracle,” and “fix.”

Do not claim that a test or health coach can diagnose a condition, provide medical advice, or guarantee an outcome. Use careful language such as “gather more information,” “organize your questions,” “consider your next step,” and “discuss personal concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.”

ACCESS-DELIVERY RULE
The first email is a transactional template. It should deliver access regardless of marketing consent. It needs usable placeholders for the actual access components because the live delivery URLs will be inserted later. Do not make up access URLs or pretend the product is hosted somewhere specific.

Use these exact placeholder tokens in the transactional email:
- {{PAID_LIBRARY_URL}}
- {{STARTER_PROTOCOL_URL}}
- {{COMPANION_GUIDE_URL}}
- {{MASTERCLASS_URL}}
- {{COMMUNITY_URL}}
- {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}

DESTINATION TOKENS FOR MARKETING EMAILS
Use only these exact destination tokens. Do not invent any URLs.
- {{PAID_LIBRARY_URL}}
- {{PAID_EPISODE_1_URL}}
- {{PAID_SERIES_CONTINUE_URL}}
- {{STARTER_PROTOCOL_URL}}
- {{TESTING_MEMBER_OFFER_URL}}
- {{NEXT_WEBINAR_OR_EVERGREEN_URL}}
- {{BUYER_NEXT_STEP_HUB_URL}}
- {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}

SEQUENCE STRATEGY

A. Transactional access delivery — immediately
Purpose: Fulfill the promise. Confirm the purchase and explain what is included. Encourage one small first action, not a binge or a total lifestyle overhaul.
Required CTA: “Open your complete protocol” → {{PAID_LIBRARY_URL}}
Include clear support language using {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

B. $199 recovery — 20 minutes after payment, only if the $199 product is absent
Purpose: Give an optional next step after the native Zipify offer has had time to resolve.
Required CTA: “Explore the member testing option” → {{TESTING_MEMBER_OFFER_URL}}
Tone: calm and educational. Explicitly say the complete protocol remains valuable on its own.

C. Day 1 — paid orientation
Purpose: Establish that the reader owns the complete protocol and does not have to do everything at once.
Required CTA: “Watch Episode 1 and open your guide” → {{PAID_EPISODE_1_URL}}
Core idea: Watch, reflect, choose one question or pattern that feels relevant.

D. Day 3 — series activation
Purpose: Encourage active use of the series. Teach the reader to pause, take one note, and return to ideas rather than passively consuming content.
Required CTA: “Continue with the next episode” → {{PAID_SERIES_CONTINUE_URL}}

E. Day 6 — protocol activation
Purpose: Introduce the 30-day protocol as a guide, not a rigid rulebook. Invite one sustainable action for the next week.
Required CTA: “Start the 30-day protocol” → {{STARTER_PROTOCOL_URL}}

F. Day 9 — testing education
Purpose: Explain, without pressure, when a person may want more information and a more individualized conversation. The offer is optional. Do not make medical claims.
Required CTA: “Explore the member testing option” → {{TESTING_MEMBER_OFFER_URL}}

G. Day 13 — live education
Purpose: Invite the reader to the current approved Interconnected webinar or evergreen teaching session. Frame it as a place to bring questions and connect the dots.
Required CTA: “Reserve your place” → {{NEXT_WEBINAR_OR_EVERGREEN_URL}}
If the destination is an evergreen replay, write the body so it works without a date or time.

H. Day 17 — decision support
Purpose: Help the buyer choose among three legitimate routes: remain self-guided, explore testing plus a health-coach conversation, or attend the live teaching. Reinforce that there is no universal right next step.
Required CTA: “Choose your next step” → {{BUYER_NEXT_STEP_HUB_URL}}

I. Day 24 — re-engagement
Purpose: Bring them back to one meaningful insight or action instead of overwhelming them with more information.
Required CTA: “Reopen your protocol” → {{PAID_LIBRARY_URL}}
Use a single reflection prompt: “What is one small change I am willing to protect for the next seven days?”

J. Day 30 — progress review and handoff
Purpose: Help the buyer reflect on the last month and invite them to the approved next live/core-funnel experience. This should feel like an invitation to continue learning, not a hard close.
Required CTA: “Join the next live session” → {{NEXT_WEBINAR_OR_EVERGREEN_URL}}

DELIVERABLE FORMAT
For each email, provide the following sections in this exact order:
1. Internal email name
2. Timing and enrollment condition
3. Subject line — give 3 options
4. Preview text — give 1 option
5. Primary CTA text and required token destination
6. Full email body in plain-text-friendly paragraphs
7. Short compliance and implementation note, if needed

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- Keep each marketing email between 180 and 330 words unless the transactional access delivery needs a little more room to explain the included assets.
- Use one primary CTA per email. You may include one low-friction support sentence, but do not add competing buttons.
- Do not use excessive headings, emojis, long lists, or all-caps emphasis.
- Do not use countdowns, deadlines, discounts, price comparisons, testimonials, invented studies, or unsupported statistics.
- Make the emails feel distinct. Do not recycle the same opening paragraph, same sentence structure, or same generic call to action.
- Do not mention a “free series” after purchase. This is a paid-owner experience.
- The only email that may mention the $199 offer by name is the 20-minute recovery email and the Day 9 / Day 17 decision-support emails.
- Do not add SMS copy.
- Do not write or recommend automation changes. Write copy only.
- At the end, provide a concise “flow QA checklist” that confirms the purchase suppression, test-buyer exit, webinar-registration handoff, and marketing-consent rule are implemented by the marketing operations team.

Return the complete sequence now. Do not ask me questions. Keep any unavailable destination as the exact placeholder token provided above.
```

## Implementation note

Approach A has been selected as the **target architecture**: a dedicated, purchase-triggered buyer lifecycle that is separate from the free-screening sequence. It should be built as a draft only after the access-delivery mechanism and actual destinations are verified. The existing free-screening flow should eventually receive a purchase-based exit or per-message buyer filter so customers are not treated as free viewers after paying.

The current single $199 buyer email should become the delayed, purchase-suppressed fallback described in the prompt. It should not be removed until the native Zipify path has been observed on a real eligible order and the fallback timing has been approved.

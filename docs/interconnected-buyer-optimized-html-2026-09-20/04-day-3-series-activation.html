There's a difference between watching something and using it.

Most people watch health content the way they watch anything else -- passively, half-distracted, and then it's gone. Nothing changes, and they blame themselves.

Try this instead. When you watch the next episode, keep one page open. Every time something makes you go "huh -- that's me," pause and write one sentence. Not a summary. Just the thing that felt personal.

By the end of the series you won't have pages of notes. You'll have a short, honest list of the patterns that actually apply to you. That list is worth more than the whole nine hours, because it's the beginning of your own map.

Health isn't a subject to study. It's a practice to run. This is how you start running it.

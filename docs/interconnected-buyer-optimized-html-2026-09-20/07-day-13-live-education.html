You don't need to prepare anything. Just come with the one thread you've been pulling since Day 1 -- the pattern you noticed, the symptom you've wondered about, the change you're trying to make stick -- and let's put it in context.

Reading and watching build knowledge. Sitting with a teacher and other people on the same path builds understanding. This is the second kind.

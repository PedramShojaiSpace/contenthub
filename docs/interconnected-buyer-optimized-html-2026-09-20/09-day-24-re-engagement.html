If the last couple of weeks got busy and the protocol slipped down your list -- that's normal, and you're not behind. Life gets loud. The work is simply coming back.

So don't reopen everything. Don't re-watch the series or restart the whole plan. Just come back to one question:

What is one small change I am willing to protect for the next seven days?

That's it. Not five changes. Not a perfect week. One thing, protected. Maybe it's the first meal of the day. Maybe it's one food you set aside. Maybe it's lights-out thirty minutes earlier.

Protect one thing for seven days and you rebuild the muscle that makes all the rest possible. Consistency beats intensity -- it's not close.

# Interconnected Episode 5 — Revised Launch Offer Ladder

**Status:** Planning only; no live price, offer, checkout, page, traffic, or routing change.  
**Purpose:** Validate a simpler offer structure for Episode 5 that continues the Interconnected experience rather than introducing an unfamiliar Upstream course.

## Recommendation

Your diagnosis is sound. The current page asks someone who has just watched an episode of **Interconnected** to purchase an **Upstream** course that has not been adequately introduced, contextualized, or earned on that page. A course can work well when it has been framed through a webinar, longer narrative, or deliberate sales sequence; it is not the natural next step for a visitor whose present context is an episode of the docu-series.

The immediate offer should be an identifiable continuation of what the viewer has already received: an extended **Interconnected** experience with a clear “what I get next” promise. Upstream can remain a separate, later-stage offer with its own education and selling mechanism rather than being forced into Episode 5.

> The principle is simple: **sell the next logical step in the experience the person is already having.** Do not ask a viewer to make a conceptual leap into an unrelated program before they have understood why it is for them.

## Recommended launch ladder

| Path | Proposed offer | Visitor it serves | Displayed price | Page role |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1. Continue the series | **Interconnected Complete Healing Protocol** — a defined extended version of the existing $67 Interconnected kit, potentially combining the complete learning experience, only verified Jeff Bland material, and only verified practical tools. | “I want the next step from this series before I am ready for testing.” | **$199** | Primary immediate offer and lowest-friction paid continuation. |
| 2. Get individual data | **Testing + consultation** — the KBMO FIT22 and Gut Barrier Test Kit plus the confirmed consultation entitlement. | “I need to understand my own data and review it with a qualified guide.” | **$399** | Diagnostic/data-first path. |
| 3. Complete launch path | **Interconnected Complete System** — the $199 protocol plus the $399 testing-and-consultation option. | “I want both my starting data and the guided learning path.” | **$499 during the launch** | Recommended choice for qualified, high-intent visitors. |
| Later, separately | **Upstream** — its own program and conversion path, ideally introduced through the webinar or a dedicated long-form page. | “I understand the Upstream framework and want the deeper program.” | **$499 if that remains the approved standalone price** | Not an Episode 5 primary offer. |

## Why this ladder is more valid

The revised core offer aligns the transaction with the visitor’s existing mental model: *“I watched Interconnected. I want more of Interconnected, plus a practical way to use it.”* That reduces the conceptual work currently required to understand Upstream and makes the page’s education-to-offer transition much more natural.

The $199 product also creates a credible middle path between free content and a $399 testing decision. Right now, the offer set moves from a free episode to a testing purchase or an unfamiliar course. The proposed ladder gives viewers a lower-risk paid way to deepen their engagement without needing to accept a new brand or an unearned clinical commitment.

The $499 path is commercially coherent only if it is presented as the **combined system**. At the proposed displayed prices, $399 for testing/consultation plus $199 for the protocol equals **$598**. A $499 launch bundle represents a clear **$99 saving**, or approximately **16.5%** off the two standalone paths. It also creates a straightforward decision for a test buyer: the full system costs **$100 more** than testing alone.

| Price logic | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Testing + consultation | $399 |
| Interconnected Complete Healing Protocol | $199 |
| Separate-path total | $598 |
| Launch-only Complete System | $499 |
| Bundle saving | $99 |
| Incremental investment from testing to full system | $100 |

This is much clearer than a bundle that appears to combine a test with a separate $299 or $499 Upstream course, especially when the course has no established place in the visitor’s immediate episode experience.

## Naming and positioning

The core product should not be called a “course” unless it truly needs to be sold as one. “Course” can feel like more homework to a person who is already overwhelmed by symptoms, conflicting information, or the complexity of health decisions. A practical working name is:

> **The Interconnected Complete Healing Protocol**  
> *The extended next step after the series: guided lessons, practical tools, and a clearer way to apply what you are learning.*

The precise promise must be tied to confirmed deliverables. If the complete docu-series is already free, the $199 offer cannot merely repeat “the full series” as its primary value. It must distinguish the paid experience in plain language, for example through an organized implementation path, expanded learning modules, verified masterclass access, guided tools, worksheets, protocol sequencing, or other genuinely incremental resources.

Avoid promising an included Jeff Bland masterclass, tools, or coaching until those assets, access rights, delivery mechanism, and customer entitlements are verified. The offer should be built from a confirmed content inventory, not from attractive but provisional bonuses.

## Page architecture under the revised ladder

The Episode 5 page should offer one simple thought after the video:

> **You have seen the bigger picture. Choose the next level of support that fits where you are right now.**

| Order | Page block | Job it performs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Video, episode takeaways, read-along guide | Completes the educational promise and creates a low-pressure micro-conversion. |
| 2 | Short transition bridge | Connects the episode to a next step without making a sweeping clinical claim. |
| 3 | Three-path selector | Lets the viewer identify as “continue learning,” “get personal data,” or “get both.” |
| 4 | $199 Protocol card | Leads with the familiar Interconnected continuation and the precise incremental deliverables. |
| 5 | $399 Testing card | Clearly explains what the test and confirmed consultation do, without overstating diagnosis or outcomes. |
| 6 | $499 Complete System card | Marks the recommended launch option and makes the $598-to-$499 calculation highly visible. |
| 7 | FAQ, authorized proof, guarantee terms, disclaimer | Resolves hesitation after—not before—the decision architecture. |

Suggested decision copy:

| Path | “Best for” line | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| $199 Protocol | “I want a guided way to keep going with what I am learning in Interconnected.” | **Continue with the Complete Healing Protocol** |
| $399 Testing | “I want personal data and a guided results review before choosing what to do next.” | **Get the test + consultation** |
| $499 Complete System | “I want the test, the learning path, and the clearest way to put both together.” | **Get the Complete System** |

## Upstream: preserve it, but separate it

This recommendation does **not** say Upstream is a weak program. It says the page does not currently do the work needed to sell it. The course should be offered in a place where visitors understand the framework, the 6R logic, why it matters, and what they will do over time.

The stronger approach is to sell Upstream through its own webinar or dedicated long-form funnel, use it as a later follow-up after the Interconnected protocol or testing experience, and retain its own $499 proposition only if that price and package are separately verified. Do not dilute Interconnected’s immediate offer by asking the viewer to first understand two programs.

## Required verification before implementation

| Item to verify | Why it is necessary |
|---|---|
| Exact contents of the current $67 Interconnected kit | Defines the genuine incremental value in the $199 protocol. |
| Whether the full series is free, paid, or access-gated | Prevents selling the same entitlement twice without a clear added-value story. |
| Jeff Bland masterclass availability, authorization, and delivery method | Prevents promising an asset that cannot be fulfilled or reused. |
| Exact tool, worksheet, guide, and bonus inventory | Ensures the $199 value stack is factual and deliverable. |
| Testing/consultation scope and whether any bundle includes one or multiple calls | Avoids duplicating or misstating a consultation entitlement. |
| $499 launch end date and the exact guarantee/terms language | Makes urgency and risk reversal specific, truthful, and consistent. |
| Confirmed product/offer IDs and approved checkout destinations | Enables a safe route map without changing live product or checkout settings prematurely. |
| Clinical and legal review of page claims | Keeps health language educational, accurate, and appropriately qualified. |

## Controlled implementation recommendation

Do not retrofit the current page live in a piecemeal way. First, define and approve the actual $199 product inventory and the exact $499 bundle inclusion list. Then build the revised page as a controlled preview—ideally the planned dedicated Interconnected subdomain pilot—so the offer hierarchy, mobile experience, claims, CTA links, and measurement tags can be reviewed as a complete unit.

After approval, route only a deliberately selected future email or campaign to the new page. Keep price, product, checkout, channel, and major traffic variables stable while testing the revised **offer architecture**. Measure the $199 Protocol, $399 Testing, and $499 Complete System paths independently; do not combine this experience test with the existing Kajabi price-test design.

## Bottom line

The $199 Interconnected continuation, $399 testing-and-consultation path, and $499 complete-system bundle are a **more coherent Episode 5 ladder** than pushing an under-explained Upstream course. It is a better narrative, a more natural buying decision, and a cleaner bundle calculation. The deciding constraint is fulfillment: the $199 protocol must have a verified, meaningfully distinct content/tool stack beyond what is already free or included in the existing $67 kit.

At $88.23 realized revenue per entry buyer, that requires **74.96** paid $67 entries, or **1.5178%** conversion from 4,939 leads. ## Since the $199 Price Change

From August 13–16, Kajabi records 59 paid $67 entries and 8 current $199 OCUS purchases. This gives a **13.5593%** take rate. At that attachment rate, revenue per paid $67 buyer is **$93.98**, and the same weekly spend/lead model would require a **1.4248%** $67 lead-to-purchase conversion rate for 1.5x ROAS. This is a scenario using the matching-week lead and spend baseline; it is not a substitute for a separately stored August 13–16 Meta spend snapshot.

## Sensitivity

Without any OCUS revenue, the current lead and spend baseline would require 98.71 paid $67 entries—**1.9987%** conversion—to reach 1.5x ROAS. The current-week OCUS layer reduces that required entry conversion by approximately 0.48 percentage points, and the post-price-change attachment scenario reduces it by approximately 0.57 percentage points.

# OuterSignal: Urban Monk Business Case and Pilot Recommendation

## Recommendation

Run a **30-day, Shopify-purchaser-only pilot**, beginning with the free trial and moving to **Starter** only if the first profiles are accurate and actionable. Do **not** buy Pro or build a Content Hub API integration at the outset. The potential is real, but the value comes from an operator acting on signals—not from enrichment data by itself.

The main near-term use is to improve retention, partnerships, and buyer-derived advertising audiences after a person has purchased through Shopify. It will not repair the opt-in funnel, decrease lead cost by itself, replace Kajabi or Klaviyo, or make a cold audience more qualified overnight.

## What It Would Do in Plain Terms

| Layer | What OuterSignal adds | Practical Urban Monk application | Revenue mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tactical | Adds a public-facing profile and possible VIP, creator, executive, or retail signal to Shopify buyers | Alert the team when an unusually valuable buyer, affiliate candidate, practitioner, retailer, or press-adjacent person purchases | A manually managed partnership, referral, wholesale, or earned-media opportunity |
| Tactical | Groups purchasers into personas and broad interests | Test one relevant post-purchase/re-engagement message against the current generic version in Klaviyo | Higher downstream purchase or repeat-purchase revenue per recipient |
| Strategic | Shows which buyer types produce higher order value, repeat rate, and lifetime value | Sharpen Interconnected, Tantra, supplement, and Academy audience hypotheses using actual buyer—not lead—data | Better creative, offer framing, customer development, and later Meta seed construction |
| Strategic | Creates an enrichment overlay across Shopify and Klaviyo | Keep Content Hub as the record of source, consent, UTM, and revenue; use OuterSignal only as a non-authoritative profile overlay | Cleaner targeting decisions without breaking attribution |

## What It Will Not Do

OuterSignal should not be treated as an automatic revenue machine. It cannot replace the current work on message-market fit, checkout conversion, email deliverability, or first-party attribution. It also should not be used on opt-in-only leads until privacy, permissible-use, and matching-policy review are complete.

The vendor claims full customer research, persona activation, VIP/B2B detection, and exports to Klaviyo, Meta, and Slack. These are vendor claims and must be validated on Urban Monk’s own buyers. Its Shopify app requests sensitive customer information, device/activity data, customer records, and all order history; the first pilot must therefore use minimum data and purchasers only. [1] [2]

## Economics

Published website pricing is $49/month for Starter and $199/month for Pro, plus $0.03 per unique customer researched; returning customers are not recharged. [1]

| Unique customers researched in a month | Starter cost | Pro cost |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | $52 | $202 |
| 500 | $64 | $214 |
| 1,000 | $79 | $229 |

At the $67 Interconnected offer price, one additional **gross-revenue** sale covers Starter’s published fee and usage through 600 new customers. This is not a profit calculation; product costs, payment fees, and any ad spend still matter. One additional $199 sale nearly covers Pro’s fixed fee before usage, but does not justify Pro on its own at normal usage.

The published sources conflict on Starter entitlements: OuterSignal’s own pricing page lists one integration and one automated playbook, while the Shopify App Store listing says unlimited playbooks and integrations. Confirm the account-specific entitlement before paying; do not choose Pro merely to obtain API access, because OuterSignal’s pricing page lists Custom API Access on Starter as well. [1] [2]

## Recommended 30-Day Pilot

1. Install the free Shopify app and inspect the first available purchaser profiles. Do not export or activate data yet.
2. Spot-check profiles against 25 buyers where the team can reasonably validate the result. Stop if false positives or vague profiles make the information unreliable.
3. Use only three controlled plays after the data passes review:
   - A manual Slack/dashboard queue for legitimate VIP, practitioner, retailer, affiliate, or partnership signals.
   - One Klaviyo purchaser segment with a persona-tailored **post-purchase or re-engagement** message versus the existing generic message. Keep offer, cadence, sender, and eligibility constant.
   - One buyer-derived Meta audience experiment only after a meaningful, clean buyer cohort exists; do not make it the primary 30-day proof point.
4. Keep current consent, UTM, order, and revenue data in Shopify/Content Hub. Do not let OuterSignal overwrite source attribution or customer consent.

## Success and Stop/Go Rules

Continue past 30 days only if at least one of these occurs: a credible partnership/referral/wholesale opportunity is created; the persona-tailored test improves **incremental revenue per recipient** without worsening unsubscribes/complaints; or buyer analysis produces a documented, testable change to paid creative or product positioning.

Stop if the profiles are materially inaccurate, the team cannot operationally follow up on alerts, enrichment use creates privacy concerns, or the tailored message fails to show an incremental advantage. Upgrade to Pro only if the validated pilot needs multiple simultaneous native integrations/playbooks or a separate Content Hub webhook/API feed. Otherwise Starter is the rational operating plan.

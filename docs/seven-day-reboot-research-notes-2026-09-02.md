# Seven-Day Reboot — Research Notes

**Purpose:** Source material for a new free seven-day Urban Monk onboarding experience. These notes do not authorize, create, or modify any email flow, page, video, subscriber record, or CTA.

## Verified brand anchors

The Urban Monk’s published positioning consistently centers a practical integration of Taoist practice, attention, health, and modern life. The public author statement presents Dr. Pedram Shojai as a New York Times bestselling author and filmmaker, Taoist abbot and Qigong master, husband and father, and explicitly rejects a guru posture in favor of helping people “find [their] way.” The official YouTube channel describes the work as helping people find more peace and vitality amid the demands of modern life; it currently lists 35.4K subscribers and 762 videos.

The most useful existing organizing frame is the Life Garden system: map priorities, direct attention to what matters, and take ownership of health, relationships, career, and dreams. This supports an onboarding sequence that teaches first and lets people choose a deeper next step, rather than using seven days as a disguised product pitch.

## Email-writing implications

The existing Email Optimizer guidance favors a recognizable moment, a reframe without blame, real context before a link, one clear next step, and a human close. Reboot emails should therefore have one daily teaching and a single “continue if this fits” option; the primary CTA should be the day’s five-minute video, not a program offer.

## Content and safety boundaries

1. Present all daily videos as education and personal perspective, not diagnosis, treatment, guaranteed outcomes, or a substitute for individual medical care.
2. Use practical, low-risk actions—such as a brief walk, an attention check, a screen boundary, or meal awareness—rather than prescriptive protocols. People with symptoms, medications, or medical concerns should seek qualified clinical guidance.
3. Use optional self-selection language. Do not infer a person’s health status or trigger a health-related funnel from an email click without explicit, consent-based automation design.
4. Before live deployment, re-verify every linked offer/page, its current content, and its consent/unsubscribe treatment. Do not include stale event dates, limited-capacity claims, or historic pricing from older content.

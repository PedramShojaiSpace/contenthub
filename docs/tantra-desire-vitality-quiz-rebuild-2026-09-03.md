# Tantra Desire & Vitality Quiz Rebuild — September 3, 2026

## Approved Scope

The owner approved replacement of the prior Taoist-led quiz at `/quiz/tantra` with a functional-medicine-led **Desire & Vitality Check-In**. The retained public route uses a five-question, one-question-at-a-time flow covering pathway selection, current focus, recovery capacity, intention, and a clinician-first safety screen. Results are immediate and educational; they do not diagnose, prescribe, or determine product eligibility.

The men’s pathway routes only to `https://shop.theurbanmonk.com/products/tantra-him`, and the women’s pathway routes only to `https://shop.theurbanmonk.com/products/tantra-her`. Uncertain or safety-review answers do not receive a product recommendation and instead receive clinician-first guidance.

## Privacy and Automation Boundary

The rebuild keeps only first-party session/completion measurement and an optional local email-record function. It removes the previous automatic Kajabi contact/tag activity, Klaviyo push, Gmail result email, Meta CAPI event, browser pixel event, and owner notification. The optional newsletter checkbox records preference only; no automatic subscriber enrollment or outgoing email is created by this implementation.

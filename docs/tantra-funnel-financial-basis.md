# Tantra Funnel Financial Basis

## Scope

The Tantra funnel dashboard reports paid Shopify line items only for these mapped products:

- Tantra Him — Shopify product `9068203376794`
- Tantra Her — Shopify product `9068203442330`
- Tantra Bundle — Him & Her — Shopify product `9068203540634`

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Shopify Tantra Revenue | Sum of the mapped paid Shopify product line items for the selected period. |
| Meta Spend | Spend from Meta campaigns or ad sets whose names include `tantra`. |
| Shopify SKU ROAS | Shopify Tantra Revenue ÷ Meta Spend. |
| Cost per Purchase | Meta Spend ÷ paid Tantra product units. |
| Purchase Rate | Paid Tantra product units ÷ captured Tantra quiz emails. |

Revenue is intentionally Shopify-only in this section. It is not inferred from Meta pixel events and does not include Kajabi transactions, shipping, tax, refunds, or unrelated Shopify products.

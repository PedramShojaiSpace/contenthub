## Day 1 — Why He Stopped

**Subject:** The thing men rarely say out loud

**Preview text:** This is not about blame. It is about naming the distance before it hardens.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

When a man begins to pull away, it is easy for both people to tell themselves a story about what it means. She feels unwanted. He feels pressured. Both people start protecting themselves, and a small gap turns into a quiet room with two people living in it.

But the distance is often not a verdict on the relationship. It can be a signal that the conditions for connection have been getting squeezed by stress, exhaustion, unspoken resentment, health pressures, and the pace of life.

I recorded a short video about what can sit underneath that withdrawal—and why the first move is not accusation. It is curiosity.

## Day 2 — Sex Is the Flower

**Subject:** Sex is the flower. The relationship is the garden.

**Preview text:** Desire rarely disappears in one place. It is usually part of a larger picture.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

In the West, we often treat sex as a scorecard. Are we having enough? Is it working? Who is at fault?

That framing misses the point.

Sex is the flower. It reflects what has been happening in the soil: rest, trust, tenderness, physical health, emotional safety, the small ways we show up for each other, and the willingness to keep choosing the relationship when life gets loud.

That does not mean every problem is solved by romance. It means that when the flower is struggling, we do not only stare at the flower. We tend the whole garden.

**See the video here:** [Sex Is the Flower](https://content.theurbanmonk.com/tantra/sex-is-the-flower?utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=tantra_9_day_video_nurture&utm_content=d02_sex_is_the_flower)

Let this be a different kind of conversation with your partner: not “what is wrong with us?” but “what does our relationship need more of?”

Dr. Pedram Shojai

## Day 3 — The Love Bank

**Subject:** The relationship needs deposits before the next rough patch

**Preview text:** Regular connection can lengthen the fuse when life becomes difficult.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

Every long relationship has rough patches. Work gets intense. Parents age. Kids need more. Bodies change. Bills arrive. Something eventually presses on both people at once.

What determines whether a couple gets through those seasons is not whether they ever argue. It is whether they have made enough deposits into the love bank before the withdrawal comes.

Small moments of affection, repair, laughter, touch, attention, and making love are not luxuries. They are the reserves that help a relationship remain generous when life is not.

**See the video here:** [The Love Bank](https://content.theurbanmonk.com/tantra/love-bank?utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=tantra_9_day_video_nurture&utm_content=d03_love_bank)

Consider one small deposit you could make this week without making it a grand gesture.

Dr. Pedram Shojai

## Day 4 — The King and Queen

**Subject:** When the King and Queen lose the field

**Preview text:** The energy of a home changes when the partnership is no longer being protected.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

In the old traditions, the King and Queen were not titles of dominance. They were a picture of two people protecting the field of their home together.

When that field is cared for, outside pressure does not get to define the whole relationship. When it collapses, every demand has a direct line into the marriage: stress, screens, conflict, fatigue, family obligations, and old resentments.

The work is not to become perfect. The work is to come back to the center—to remember that you are on the same side and that the relationship deserves its own protected space.

**See the video here:** [The King and Queen](https://content.theurbanmonk.com/tantra/king-and-queen?utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=tantra_9_day_video_nurture&utm_content=d04_king_and_queen)

If this speaks to you, share the video with your partner and ask one question: “What would help us protect our field this week?”

## Day 5 — Considering Divorce?

**Subject:** Before you decide it is over, pause here

**Preview text:** A hard season deserves a clear-eyed look before a permanent decision.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

Some couples arrive at a frightening sentence: “I do not know if I can do this anymore.”

That sentence should be taken seriously. It should not be minimized, romanticized, or answered with a slogan. But it can also be worth asking whether the relationship is truly finished—or whether two depleted people have lost access to the connection that brought them together.

There are times when separation is necessary and protective. There are also times when a relationship needs a real pause, an honest inventory, and a new way of meeting each other before a final decision is made.

**See the video here:** [Considering Divorce?](https://content.theurbanmonk.com/tantra/considering-divorce?utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=tantra_9_day_video_nurture&utm_content=d05_considering_divorce)

No pressure. Just give the question the attention it deserves.

Dr. Pedram Shojai

## Day 6 — Why She Stopped Wanting To

**Subject:** Why she may have stopped wanting to

**Preview text:** Desire does not usually vanish for no reason. It often goes quiet under too much pressure.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

For many women, desire does not disappear because they have stopped loving their partner. It can go quiet under the weight of caregiving, pressure, interrupted sleep, physical changes, resentment, unspoken needs, and years of intimacy that has not made enough room for her experience.

The answer is not to pressure her back into wanting. It is to create the conditions where she can feel seen, safe, rested, and genuinely desired again.

Tantric principles offer a useful reframe: sensuality is not a side note, and neither is her pleasure. The relationship changes when both people become students of connection again.

**See the video here:** [Why She Stopped Wanting To](https://content.theurbanmonk.com/tantra/why-she-stopped?utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=tantra_9_day_video_nurture&utm_content=d06_why_she_stopped)

Watch this one with compassion, not defensiveness.

## Day 7 — The Female Orgasm

**Subject:** The missing ingredient in Western sexuality

**Preview text:** A better sex life begins when her experience is no longer an afterthought.

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

Many couples have inherited a model of sex that is rushed, performance-driven, and built around a narrow definition of success.

That model leaves a great deal of intimacy on the table.

In Tantra, her pleasure is not an optional bonus. It is central to the exchange. Slowing down, listening, learning her language of touch, and letting sensuality lead can change the emotional atmosphere of a relationship—not because there is a trick, but because both people are finally meeting each other more fully.

**See the video here:** [The Female Orgasm: The Missing Ingredient in Western Sexuality](https://content.theurbanmonk.com/tantra/female-orgasm?utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=tantra_9_day_video_nurture&utm_content=d07_female_orgasm)

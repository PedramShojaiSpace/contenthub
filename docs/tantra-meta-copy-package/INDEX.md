## Shared Operating Standard

The common naming, audience, policy, measurement, and creative specification is in [README.md](./README.md). The campaign taxonomy is date-stamped and includes each destination slug; all packages use US-only broad targeting for adults 35–65+, Traffic with Landing Page Views, and an initial **Learn More** CTA. The performance targets are 2%+ outbound CTR and 35%+ page-to-quiz opt-in rate; they are targets rather than promises.

## Pre-Launch Checklist

Before any future build, confirm that the relevant Wistia video is live on the exact page, copy the precise destination UTM string from the package, create the Meta object in paused state only, and review the content against the final creative. Do not reactivate the old direct-to-quiz Tantra ads as a substitute for the planned video-page packages.

By the time we finished, the room had shifted. People smiled in a way you could feel in your chest.

This is the heart of King and Queen: practical attention, simple repair, and shared leadership. Over the years I’ve worked with households that were stuck in patterns of withdrawal and reactivity. Rituals don’t have to be ornate; their power is in consistency and intention.

I use stories from my own household and from the people I coach to show how these practices land. We explore how to make repair immediate and kind, how to ask for attention without accusation, and how to rotate leadership so everyone feels seen.

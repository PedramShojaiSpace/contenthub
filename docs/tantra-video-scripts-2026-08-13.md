## How to Use These Scripts

Each script is designed for a 3–5 minute talking-head video recorded in your usual style — no teleprompter required, just the outline and key phrases. After recording, the video gets embedded at the top of a blog post on `theurbanmonk.com`. The blog post includes a brief written intro (2–3 paragraphs), the video, a short written summary of the key points, and then the Tantra quiz embedded at the bottom with a line like: *"Take the 2-minute quiz below to find the right path for your situation."*

The ad links directly to the blog post. The visitor reads or watches, feels seen and understood, and then takes the quiz already warm.

## Production Notes

**Recording:** Talking-head, natural light or studio, no teleprompter needed. The scripts are guides, not word-for-word reads. Speak in your own words — the structure is what matters.

**Length:** Aim for 3–5 minutes per video. Under 3 minutes feels rushed for this topic. Over 6 minutes loses cold traffic.

**Tone:** Calm, direct, authoritative but not clinical. You're a doctor who has seen this a thousand times and genuinely wants to help. Not a salesperson.

**Thumbnail:** Each video should have a thumbnail with your face, a short text hook (e.g., "Why He Stopped Wanting To"), and a clean background. No stock photos of couples.

**Compliance note:** Do not make specific medical claims about the compounds in the video. Frame as "compounds that work on these pathways" and "30-day protocol" rather than "this will treat X condition." The written blog post can include the published research citations.

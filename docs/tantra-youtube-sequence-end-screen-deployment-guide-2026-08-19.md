**Audience:** YouTube VA / channel operator  
**Prepared for:** The Urban Monk  
**Purpose:** Guide viewers through the seven Tantra videos as one coherent relationship-reconnection journey, then move qualified viewers to the Tantra quiz.

## The Strategic Sequence

The sequence should begin with the highest-stakes entry point—relationship distress—then remove blame by addressing both partners, teach a more attentive model of intimacy, widen the frame to the relationship and the body, and close with a sustainable practice plus a personal next step. The aim is not to force a sale from a cold viewer. It is to earn the next view, deepen trust, and offer the quiz only after the viewer has received a useful arc of education.

Use the playlist as the secondary end-screen destination on Videos 1–6. It gives a viewer an alternative to the next specific video while keeping the viewer inside the intended journey.

## Standard YouTube Setup Rules for Every Video

Each video should be set as **not made for kids** if that accurately reflects the channel’s audience and intended content classification. End screens and cards are unavailable on videos set as made for kids. Use a 20-second end screen where the source video leaves enough open visual space; YouTube permits end screens in the final 5–20 seconds and allows up to four elements on standard 16:9 videos. Cards are suppressed while end screens are shown. [1] [2]

For Videos 1–6, use this same three-element end-screen layout during the final 20 seconds:

| Element | Destination | Placement | On-screen label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary video | The exact next video in the sequence | Largest element; left or center, where the video does not cover Dr. Pedram | **WATCH NEXT** |
| Playlist | **Tantra: Reconnect, Restore, and Rebuild** playlist | Smaller element, upper right | **WATCH THE FULL SERIES** |
| Subscribe | The Urban Monk channel | Smaller element, lower right | **SUBSCRIBE FOR MORE** |

For Video 7, use this final layout during the final 20 seconds:

| Element | Destination | Placement | On-screen label |

## VA Deployment Checklist

1. Confirm all seven videos are public or scheduled and are not marked made for kids.
2. Create the playlist and add all seven videos in the listed order.
3. Add the standard 20-second end screen to Videos 1–6, selecting the exact next video rather than “best for viewer.”
4. Add one mid-video card to Videos 1–6 that points to the exact next video; use the timing guidance above rather than placing it in the final 20 seconds.
5. Add the final CTA end screen and description/pinned-comment links to Video 7.
6. Paste the description add-on under each video’s existing description. Replace every `[VA: insert …]` placeholder with the actual YouTube video or playlist URL after the uploads are finalized.
7. Preview every end screen in YouTube Studio. Confirm the next-video elements never cover a face, subtitle, or critical on-screen text.
8. Test the public watch path as a viewer: Video 1 → Video 2 → Video 3 → Video 4 → Video 5 → Video 6 → Video 7 → quiz.
9. Check YouTube Analytics after 7 and 30 days for End screen element click rate, Cards click rate, Playlist starts, average view duration, and quiz sessions attributed to `utm_source=youtube`.

## Guardrails

Do not link from a video to a product checkout. The public YouTube viewer should first receive the full educational sequence, then be invited to the quiz. Do not describe the quiz as diagnosis or promise a medical outcome. Keep the final language practical and relational: “a personal starting point,” “identify where to begin,” and “two-minute Tantra quiz.”
